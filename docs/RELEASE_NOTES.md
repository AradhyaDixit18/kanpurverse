# Release notes: Kanpur Chronicles 1.0 (candidate, not yet in production)

- KanpurVerse is renamed **Kanpur Chronicles: City of Stories**, with a new logo and the Velto Studios studio mark.
- New protagonist **Kanupriya Tommy**, with a signature look and backstory.
- **Eight-chapter story campaign**: Kanupriya's return, the Laddoo Ka Chakkar caper, Mall Road, the railway run, the Naveen Market mystery, Ganga Ghat, the city-wide evidence run, and the mela-night finale at Parmat Ghat. Includes cinematic letterboxed cutscenes in Hindi and English and end credits.
- Side missions now unlock after Chapter 2. Free roam continues after the credits.
- The HUD shows chapter progress and save status. A caught player now returns to the checkpoint correctly.
- 184 automated tests, including a full campaign playthrough.
