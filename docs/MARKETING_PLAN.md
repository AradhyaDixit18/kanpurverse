# Marketing Plan (plan only — nothing produced or published)

Per your rule, marketing assets are made only after a playable, QA'd build exists, and nothing is published without approval. This is the plan, ready to execute with the Velto reel engine once you say go.

## Positioning
"The first open-world game where Kanpur is the hero." Local pride + nostalgia + "play with your friends in your browser, free, no download." Kanpuriya humour is the hook; the voice lines are the share-bait.

## Audience
Kanpur natives (in Kanpur and the diaspora in Delhi/NCR, Bengaluru, Mumbai, abroad), UP college students, Hindi gaming creators, "Kanpuriya" meme pages.

## Launch sequence (after approval)
1. **Teaser reel (Instagram @velto_studios, 15 s):** gameplay capture driving an auto down Mall Road at sunset, Raju's line "meter se nahi, dil se chalenge", end card "KanpurVerse — browser mein, free. Link in bio." Built with `engines/` (Velto reel pipeline), Neerja voice for the narration, Kanpuriya NPC audio from the game.
2. **"Pehchaan to?" carousel:** 6 screenshots of landmarks, caption asks people to name them — comments drive reach.
3. **Founder LinkedIn post (Velto profile):** "We built a multiplayer open world of our city in the browser — tech stack, what we learned" (developer audience, hiring/clients angle).
4. **Creator seeding:** 10 Kanpur meme/vlog pages with a DM template (from `SOCIAL_MEDIA/OUTREACH/`), offer early room names for their followers.
5. **Community:** a shared room name per college ("hbti", "csjmu", "iitk") announced in posts; weekly "Sunset at Jheel" meet-up at 18:00 IST.

## Assets to produce (not yet)
Gameplay capture script (OBS, 1080×1920 crop), 3 reels, 1 carousel, 3 stories, landing OG image (1200×630), 30-second trailer with music from `engines/audio/synth.py`.

## Metrics
Visits → Play clicks → characters created → 5-minute sessions → mission completions → D1/D7 return. All from server logs + Vercel analytics (free, cookieless) — no third-party trackers.

## Guardrails
No paid ads without approval; no fake reviews; no impersonating Kanpur institutions; landmark disclaimer in every bio link.
